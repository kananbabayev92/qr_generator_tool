Privacy Policy for QR Tool

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the QR Tool mobile application (the "Application") for Android devices. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Application.

1. Information Collection and Core Features

We respect your privacy. QR Tool does NOT collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable personal information (such as your name, email, or contacts) on external servers. All QR scanning and generation operations are processed entirely on your local device.

2. Device Permissions and Usage

To provide core features, the Application requests the following permissions:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Required strictly for real-time scanning of QR codes and barcodes using your device camera. The camera feed is processed in memory on your device. No photos, videos, or camera recordings are ever stored or transmitted to any server.
  • Photo Library / Storage Access: Used exclusively when you choose to import an image from your gallery to decode a QR code, or when you choose to save an exported QR code image to your device storage. We do not access or collect any other photos or files.

3. Advertisements and Third-Party Services

This Application displays advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob). In order to display advertisements, these third-party providers may collect and process certain non-personal device information, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Device identifiers, IP address, and operating system information
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data

This data is used exclusively to serve, personalize, and measure advertisements. For more information on how Google uses data when you use our app, please visit: Google Advertising & Privacy Policies.

4. Local Data Storage

Scan history, generated QR codes, and application settings are stored strictly locally on your device (using local storage mechanisms like SharedPreferences). This data remains on your physical hardware and can be cleared at any time via device settings or by uninstalling the application.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Security

We value your trust in using our Application. Since personal data is not collected or transmitted to external servers by our app, your information remains private and secure on your device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.
