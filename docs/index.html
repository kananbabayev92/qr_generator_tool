OFFICIAL PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy for Ontero QR

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Ontero QR mobile application (the "Application") for Android devices, developed and published by Ontero. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Application.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection (Zero-Knowledge)

We believe in fundamental privacy. Ontero QR does NOT collect, transmit, sell, or rent any personally identifiable information (such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, precise geolocation, contacts, or photos) to external servers. All QR code decoding and generation operations are processed entirely on your local device.

2. Device Permissions and Usage

To provide core features, the Application requests only the minimal necessary permissions:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Required strictly for real-time scanning of QR codes and barcodes using your device camera. The video feed is analyzed in-memory. No photos, videos, or camera recordings are ever stored or transmitted to any server.
  • Photo Library / Gallery Access: Used exclusively when you deliberately choose to pick an image from your gallery to decode a QR code, or when saving/sharing an exported QR code image. We do not access or collect any other media files.
  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Used exclusively when you tap to open an external web link (URL) in your preferred browser, or for optional diagnostic/ad performance requests.

3. Local Data Storage

Scan history, generated QR codes, and application settings (theme, language) are stored strictly locally on your device (using local storage mechanisms like SharedPreferences). This data remains exclusively on your hardware and can be cleared at any time via in-app settings (Settings > Storage & Data > Clear) or by clearing device app data.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Application is intended for a general audience and does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Built-in Security

Ontero QR includes built-in security auditing to safeguard users from malicious file downloads (.apk, .exe, etc.) and dangerous URL scripts. Since your data is not transmitted to external servers, your information remains private and secure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date.
