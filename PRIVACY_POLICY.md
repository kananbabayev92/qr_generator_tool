# Privacy Policy for QR Tool

**Last updated:** September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the **QR Tool** mobile application (the "Application") for Android devices. This page describes how the Application handles user data, device permissions, and advertisements.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Core Features
We respect your privacy. **QR Tool does NOT collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable personal information (such as your name, email, or contacts) on external servers.** All QR scanning and generation operations are processed entirely on your local device.

### 2. Device Permissions and Usage
To provide core features, the Application requests the following permissions:
- **Camera (ndroid.permission.CAMERA):** Required strictly for real-time scanning of QR codes and barcodes using your device camera. The camera feed is processed in memory on your device. No photos, videos, or camera recordings are ever stored or transmitted to any server.
- **Photo Library / Storage Access:** Used exclusively when you choose to import an image from your gallery to decode a QR code, or when you choose to save an exported QR code image to your device storage. We do not access or collect any other photos or files.

### 3. Advertisements and Third-Party Services
This Application displays advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners (such as **Google AdMob**). To serve advertisements, these partners may automatically collect and process certain device information, such as:
- Google Advertising ID (AAID)
- IP address, device type, and operating system information
- Ad interaction and performance analytics

This data is used solely to deliver, measure, and prevent fraud in advertisements. You can learn more about how Google handles ad data at: [https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

### 4. Local Data Storage
Scan history, generated QR codes, and application settings are stored strictly locally on your device (using local storage mechanisms like SharedPreferences). This data can be cleared at any time via device settings or by uninstalling the application.

### 5. Children's Privacy
Our Application is intended for a general audience and does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

### 6. Security
Since personal data is not collected or transmitted to external servers by our app, your information remains private and secure on your physical device.

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.
